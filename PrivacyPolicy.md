# TempSnap Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the TempSnap Chrome Extension handles your data.

## What Data is Collected
TempSnap captures screenshots of the active browser tab when manually triggered by the user (via the extension popup or keyboard shortcut).

## How it is Used
The captured screenshots are temporarily stored locally to allow you to easily copy and paste them into other applications or websites.

## How Long it is Stored
Screenshot data is stored temporarily. By default, it is automatically deleted after 10 minutes. Users can adjust this auto-delete timer between 1 minute and 24 hours in the extension's settings.

## Does it Leave the User Device?
**No.** All captured screenshots and associated data are stored strictly within your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). TempSnap does not transmit, upload, or share any of your data with external servers, tracking services, or third parties. It operates entirely offline on your local machine.
